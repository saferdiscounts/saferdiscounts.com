## Priority edits / teaching notes

| Portuguese | Current concern | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| sou / somos / são | Describing ser as always permanent is misleading | Teach identity, classification, characteristics and context; avoid permanent/temporary absolute rule |
| estou / está / estamos / estão | Describing estar as always temporary is misleading | Teach states and location with contextual examples |
| quis | Existing Arabic includes first and third person, but is ambiguous without a subject | Label 'أردتُ / أراد / أرادت بحسب الفاعل' |
| me levanto | 'أستيقظ' is not always equivalent to levantar-se | Prefer 'أنهض من الفراش'; acordar = يستيقظ |
| às | Time example needs an explicit explanation | Explain contraction a + as and 'às oito' = الساعة الثامنة |
| tão... como | Formula lacks context | Add 'tão alto como' = بنفس الطول |
| ei, á, emos, ão | Isolated suffixes risk confusion in listening exercises | Present as written suffixes of future tense with falar: falarei, falará, falaremos, falarão; verify whether actual audio says punctuation |
| suco de coco | Arabic reading passage translates it as 'ماء جوز الهند' | Verify intent: suco de coco = عصير جوز الهند; água de coco = ماء جوز الهند. Do not change audio blindly |
| ser / estar conjugations | Forms repeated in basic and advanced | Intentional repetition is acceptable if level purpose and audio links match |
| numbers 21–99 | No dedicated items for each number in index | Teach construction with 'vinte e um', 'trinta e cinco', 'noventa e nove' without claiming audio exists |
